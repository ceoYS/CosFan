# Reference: Instagram - Verified creator broadcast with reactions & poll
- source_app: Instagram (iOS)
- mobbin_category: Messaging / Broadcast channels
- screen_type: One-to-many creator broadcast feed (text updates, photo, in-feed poll) with rich per-message reactions
- target_cosfan_screen: 1:N creator message channel (Bubble-style update feed)
- user_job: As a fan, I want to follow a creator's stream of short updates, react with an emoji, and occasionally vote in a quick poll — all without replying like a DM.
- first_visible_promise: Header "What's Good on Instagram" + verified tick + "3.4M members" with a bell (notification) icon — immediately frames a massive, official one-to-many channel I subscribe to for updates.
- primary_cta: One-tap emoji reaction on each message (reaction tray); "Vote" on poll messages
- secondary_cta: Bell/notification toggle in header; scroll-to-latest down-arrow FAB; "..." options -> Report / Leave
- section_order: 1) Header (channel name + verified + member count + bell) -> 2) Feed of creator-authored messages (text bubbles, photo cards, poll cards) with date/time dividers -> 3) Under each message, reaction tallies with large counts (heart 24.6K, +480) and a reply-arrow count -> 4) Inline poll card with options + live percentage bars + "Seen by 631.3K" -> 5) Down-arrow to jump to newest.
- trust_elements: Verified badge; very large member + reaction + "Seen by" counts as heavy social proof; official Instagram authorship; clear authored-by-creator-only feed (no fan messages interleaved).
- friction_removed: Fans never need to type — react or vote in one tap; polls lower the bar to participate vs. composing; notification bell is one tap; "Seen by" reassures the update reached others (you're not alone).
- empty_loading_error_handling: Not shown; standard chat-history load with a jump-to-latest affordance for long feeds.
- monetization_exposure: None directly; a separate onboarding screen ("Welcome to <creator>'s subscription") lists a "Subscriber broadcast channel — Read and react to messages" as a paid perk, showing how a broadcast channel can sit behind membership.
- onboarding_step: The notification bell + (elsewhere) the subscription welcome list position the channel as a recurring-engagement and potential paid-tier surface.
- mobile_pattern: Bubble + card feed; date dividers; high-count reaction tallies with "+N"; inline poll card with progress bars and avatars; jump-to-latest FAB; header bell toggle; "..." -> Report/Leave sheet.
- why_relevant_to_cosfan: Demonstrates the BROADCAST-ONLY feed at scale — every message is creator-authored, fans only react/vote, which is exactly the safe 1:N model CosFan needs. The poll card is a great low-effort engagement primitive: it persuades fans to participate (placed inline mid-feed) and reduces the friction/fear of "what do I even say." Reaction tallies + "Seen by" drive a sense of a living community without any live/call mechanic.
- what_to_learn: Two reusable primitives: (1) inline poll/question card as a lightweight fan action, (2) the Report/Leave options sheet for member safety (clean, minimal: just Report and Leave). The high-count social proof is reusable conceptually (CosFan can show reaction counts and a soft "Seen by N"). Must-modify: tone down to pastel, smaller realistic counts for an early-stage app, and keep authorship strictly creator-only.
- what_not_to_copy: Exact emoji tray, IG brand, exact poll styling/colors, and the "members" wording. Do NOT copy the adjacent LIVE video broadcast screen from the same app (live banner, mic/camera controls, "started a live video", "join your video") — that violates CosFan's no-live/no-call rule.
- risk_notes: AVOID — the same Instagram channel surface also exposes LIVE video and a "subscriber social channel ... chat with <creator> when they start a social channel with a small group" (group live chat). Both were EXCLUDED as references. CosFan must not surface live, mic/camera, or "small group chat with creator" affordances. Keep only the asynchronous text/photo/poll + reaction layer. No presence dots.
- MVP_action: Add an inline "poll / quick question" card type to the broadcast feed as the primary fan-participation primitive beyond reactions (one tap to vote, show pastel percentage bars). Implement a minimal "..." -> Report / Leave sheet for safety. Show reaction counts and an optional soft "Seen by" line. Explicitly scope OUT any live, mic/camera, or group-call entry points.
