# Reference: Discord - Announcement channel (read-only broadcast)
- source_app: Discord (iOS)
- mobbin_category: Messaging / Community channels
- screen_type: Read-only announcement channel — admins/officials broadcast, members read (and follow into their own servers)
- target_cosfan_screen: 1:N creator message channel (broadcast-only update feed)
- user_job: As a member, I want to read official broadcast posts from the people who run this space, knowing it's announcements-only and not a back-and-forth chat.
- first_visible_promise: Channel header "#moderator-only" with a megaphone-style "#" and a small online count; the body is a long official "Community Updates" post by a verified "OFFICIAL" author — signals an authoritative, read-mostly broadcast channel.
- primary_cta: Read the announcement (consume); on the settings side, the "Announcement Channel" toggle + "Follow"/"Publish" model lets posts reach other servers
- secondary_cta: Channel settings expose "Announcement Channel" toggle with explainer ("Users can Follow this channel... Posts you 'Publish' from this channel will appear in those servers"); pinned messages; notification settings
- section_order: 1) Channel header (name + small presence count) -> 2) System/info callout (e.g. "Activity Alerts Enabled") -> 3) Official broadcast message block: author + verified OFFICIAL badge + timestamp + multi-paragraph announcement -> 4) Subsequent system/automod lines -> 5) (Settings screen) Announcement Channel toggle + Follow/Publish explainer + Pinned Messages + Notification Settings.
- trust_elements: "OFFICIAL" verified badge on the broadcasting account; system callouts clearly labeled; channel-level explainer text describing exactly how publish/follow works; pinned messages for canonical info.
- friction_removed: Members don't have to participate — it's read-first; the Follow/Publish model lets one authored post propagate to many followers' spaces without re-posting (true one-to-many distribution); notification settings let members tune noise.
- empty_loading_error_handling: A "new messages since <time>" banner and a "NEW MESSAGES" divider with jump arrows help members catch up; threads-only channels show a "This is a threads only channel" footer with a single Create Thread CTA.
- monetization_exposure: None — announcement/broadcast is a free community primitive here.
- onboarding_step: The "Set up the basics -> Community Updates Channel / Rules channel" setup step shows how an announcement/broadcast channel is designated during server onboarding.
- mobile_pattern: Chat-style read feed with system callouts; verified author badges; "Announcement Channel" toggle with Follow/Publish distribution model; pinned messages; new-message catch-up banner; threads-only footer CTA.
- why_relevant_to_cosfan: Strong model for the READ-ONLY nature of CosFan's channel — it proves that a broadcast surface can suppress fan composing entirely and still feel alive via system callouts and pinned info. The author's OFFICIAL badge PERSUADES (authoritative source), placed at the top of each post to reduce "is this really from the creator?" doubt. The Follow/Publish distribution concept maps to CosFan creators broadcasting once to all their followers.
- what_to_learn: Reusable: (1) a clearly-attributed, verified author block per broadcast post; (2) pinned message for the channel's "start here" info; (3) a catch-up affordance ("N new since <time>") for fans returning to a long feed; (4) read-only posture as a default. Must-modify: Discord's density and dark chrome -> CosFan's white-first pastel; simplify the publish/follow plumbing to a single "broadcast to my followers" action.
- what_not_to_copy: Discord brand, "#"/server metaphor, role/permission complexity, and the dark theme. Do NOT copy any voice-channel or "Public VC"/"Stream Room"/"AFK" entries that appear in Discord's channel lists. Avoid copying the multi-server sidebar mental model — CosFan is a single creator -> fans relationship, not nested servers.
- risk_notes: AVOID — sibling Discord screens (channel-list views) prominently show VOICE CHANNELS, "Public VC", "Stream Room", "Hangout", AFK with mic-muted icons, and member presence under voice rooms. Those were EXCLUDED. Also several Discord/related screens show ONLINE/OFFLINE/"Active now" presence and green dots — CosFan must NOT adopt presence indicators or any voice/stream channel. Use only the read-only announcement post + pinned + catch-up patterns.
- MVP_action: Make the creator channel read-only by default: render each broadcast as an attributed post (creator avatar + verified/creator badge + timestamp), support a pinned "start here / welcome" post, and add a lightweight "new since you last visited" catch-up marker. Provide notification settings (all / highlights / off). Strip every voice/stream/VC concept and all presence/online indicators. Fans engage only via reactions (and optional comments per the Instagram/Cameo refs).
