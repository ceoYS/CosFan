# Reference: Calm - Paywall with honest renewal disclosure + pre-renewal reminder
- source_app: Calm
- mobbin_category: Subscription / Billing — paywall / free-trial subscribe sheet
- screen_type: Plan-selection paywall sheet with explicit renewal terms and a "remind me before renewal" toggle
- target_cosfan_screen: Subscription / paywall sheet — the honest-billing variant (trial + transparent recurring terms)
- user_job: "Let me start, but tell me the truth about what I'll be charged and when, and warn me before the trial converts."
- first_visible_promise: "Your plan is ready. Unlock Calm" with stacked plan cards (Individual yearly with "Save 56%", Family, Monthly) — and crucially a bottom block that states the full conversion math in plain words: "Totally free for 7 days, then $7.67/month, billed annually at $91.98/year. Cancel anytime."
- primary_cta: "Try Free & Subscribe" — single full-width button; the honest terms sit directly beneath it, in the same eyeline, not on a separate fine-print page.
- secondary_cta: "View Fewer Plans" expander and an "Enable notifications... Open Settings" prompt tied to the reminder toggle; not a competing colored CTA.
- section_order: (1) Close + "Your plan is ready" → (2) "7-Day Free Trial" label → (3) plan cards each with per-month + per-year + honest savings ("Save 56%", "Save up to 90%") → (4) "No Free Trial" Monthly option shown plainly alongside → (5) primary "Try Free & Subscribe" → (6) "Remind me 2 days before renewal" toggle → (7) full plain-language conversion terms + "Cancel anytime".
- trust_elements: The standout pattern — a "Remind me 2 days before renewal" toggle so the trial-to-paid conversion is not a silent surprise; full conversion sentence (free duration → then price → billing cadence → annual total); honest savings percentages with per-month normalization; "Cancel anytime"; the no-trial Monthly option shown rather than hidden.
- friction_removed: The user does not have to dig for "what happens after the trial" — it's one sentence under the button; the renewal reminder removes the classic "I forgot and got charged" resentment; per-month math is pre-computed for every plan.
- empty_loading_error_handling: If notifications are off, an inline "Enable notifications in Settings to receive your reminder — Open Settings" hint appears, so the reminder promise isn't silently broken.
- monetization_exposure: High (it's a paywall) but this is the honest end of the spectrum: the reminder toggle and full terms actively work AGAINST surprise charges, trading a bit of conversion for trust — the right trade for a fan-facing brand.
- onboarding_step: Typically first paywall after value is shown; can be the trial-start sheet.
- mobile_pattern: Stacked price cards with per-month normalization + honest savings badge; sticky primary CTA; pre-renewal reminder toggle; plain conversion-terms block under the CTA; settings-deep-link fallback.
- why_relevant_to_cosfan: This screen is the answer to CosFan's "honest billing (clear price, renewal date, easy cancel)" requirement at the moment of subscribing. It "says" the entire deal — trial length, post-trial price, billing cadence, annual total, cancel-anytime — in one eyeline, and the "remind me before renewal" toggle is a trust feature CosFan should adopt verbatim (it's the single best anti-dark-pattern affordance for trials). Persuasion (savings %, "plan is ready") is paired with radical transparency, which fits the cute-but-trustworthy collectible OS positioning.
- what_to_learn: Put the full conversion sentence in the same eyeline as the subscribe button; offer a "remind me before my trial/renewal" toggle; normalize every plan to per-month and show honest savings; show the no-trial monthly option openly; provide a settings fallback if notifications are off.
- what_not_to_copy: Calm's deep-blue brand gradient and serene-imagery treatment; its slightly aggressive "Save up to 90%" framing on Family (keep savings claims truthful for CosFan's scale). Do not copy the exact composition or wording — adapt the reminder-toggle + terms-in-eyeline structure only.
- risk_notes: Savings percentages must be mathematically real for CosFan's actual prices or it becomes a deceptive claim. The renewal reminder must actually fire (wire the notification) — promising and not delivering is worse than not offering. Keep "Cancel anytime" honest re: store billing. Avoid making the trial card pre-selected if it auto-charges without the user understanding.
- MVP_action: Build the trial paywall variant with: plan cards (per-month price + honest annual savings) → "Start free trial" pastel CTA → "Remind me before my trial ends" toggle (mint) → plain terms line "Free for X days, then $Y/month billed yearly ($Z/yr). Cancel anytime in Settings." → notifications-off fallback hint. Wire the reminder notification. Show monthly (no-trial) option openly.
