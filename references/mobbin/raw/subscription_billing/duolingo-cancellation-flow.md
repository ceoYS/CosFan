# Reference: Duolingo - Cancellation flow (honest cancel + reason survey + easy re-subscribe)
- source_app: Duolingo
- mobbin_category: Subscription / Billing — cancellation flow
- screen_type: Multi-step cancel flow (reason survey → honest consequence prompt → end-date confirmation → re-subscribe affordance)
- target_cosfan_screen: Cancellation flow (honest, clear, one decision per screen, easy to come back)
- user_job: "Let me cancel cleanly, understand when access ends, and not feel trapped — while telling you why if I want to."
- first_visible_promise: Step asks a single honest question — "Why are you cancelling Super?" — with plain radio options (don't use it anymore / subscribed by accident / out of my price range / technical issues / Other). It collects feedback without obstructing the exit.
- primary_cta: Per step, one clear action: "CONTINUE" on the survey; on the consequence prompt the honest pair "KEEP SUPER" (stay) vs "CANCEL SUPER" (proceed) where the destructive action is plainly available, not hidden.
- secondary_cta: The "stay" option is offered once, calmly ("KEEP SUPER" / "STAY ON SUPER"), not repeated across many screens. After cancel, "Renew: $83.99/year" is shown as an easy, low-pressure way back.
- section_order: (1) Reason survey ("Why are you cancelling?") + Continue → (2) honest consequence prompt ("If you cancel, you'll start seeing ads again") with Keep / Cancel → (3) App Store / system confirmation → (4) post-cancel status card: "You've cancelled your subscription. Your subscription ends on July 25." + "Renew: $83.99/year" + "About Subscriptions and Privacy".
- trust_elements: Plain end-date sentence ("Your subscription ends on July 25."), honest statement of what is lost ("you'll start seeing ads again" — not sugar-coated, not catastrophized), reason survey that respects the user's choice to leave, transparent re-subscribe price, link to subscription/privacy info.
- friction_removed: One decision per screen (no stacked guilt walls); the cancel action is always visibly present alongside "keep"; the end-date is stated so the user knows their remaining access; re-subscribing is a single labeled button — coming back is as easy as leaving.
- empty_loading_error_handling: Post-cancel state is an explicit card (not a dead end) showing cancelled status, access-end date, and a Renew button, so a user who changes their mind has an obvious, immediate path back.
- monetization_exposure: Retention is attempted ONCE via an honest trade-off ("you'll see ads again") and a clear re-subscribe price — no scarcity timers, no pre-checked boxes, no hidden cancel, no "call to cancel". This is the ethical-retention reference.
- onboarding_step: Triggered from Manage Subscription; not onboarding. (Related trial-cancel variant: "Going so soon? We'll remind you 2 days before your free trial ends" — a fair pre-trial-end reminder rather than a trap.)
- mobile_pattern: Stepwise cancel with one-question reason survey; honest consequence sheet with paired Keep/Cancel; plain end-date confirmation; persistent re-subscribe card.
- why_relevant_to_cosfan: This is CosFan's blueprint for HONEST cancellation — explicitly called out in the group mandate. Each screen "says" one thing, the consequence is stated truthfully, the exit is never hidden, and the end-date + easy renew remove the fear of "if I cancel I lose everything forever / I'll be punished." Persuasion to stay is limited to one fair trade-off, which protects CosFan's fan-trust and avoids the dark-pattern reputation that would be lethal for a subculture B2C brand. Reusable as the canonical cancel sequence.
- what_to_learn: Keep one decision per screen; offer "stay" exactly once with an honest trade-off, not a guilt maze; always state the access-end date plainly; make re-subscribe a single obvious button; optionally collect a reason but never gate the exit behind it; for trials, give a fair pre-end reminder instead of a silent charge.
- what_not_to_copy: Duolingo's mascot guilt imagery, neon gradients, and gamified tone; CosFan's consequence copy must be in its own calm voice (e.g. "If you cancel, you'll lose your member feed, member channel, and won't receive this month's photocard"). Don't adopt the "JUST A BIT LONGER..." style nudge.
- risk_notes: The dark-pattern line is sharpest here — do NOT add multiple retention screens, pre-checked re-subscribe, hidden/greyed cancel buttons, or off-app "contact us to cancel" steps. If store billing governs, route to the store's cancel sheet honestly and tell the user that's where it completes. Consequence copy must be truthful (no overstating loss to scare them into staying).
- MVP_action: Build cancel as: (optional) 1-screen reason survey → 1 honest consequence sheet ("If you cancel, you'll lose access to <creator>'s member content at the end of your billing period" + Keep / Cancel) → store/system confirm → post-cancel card ("Membership ends <date> — you keep access until then" + "Resume membership"). No more than these steps. White/pastel, calm copy.
