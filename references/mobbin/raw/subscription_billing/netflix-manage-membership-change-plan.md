# Reference: Netflix - Manage membership (change plan / pause / cancel)
- source_app: Netflix
- mobbin_category: Subscription / Billing — manage membership
- screen_type: "Manage your membership" hub (Change plan / Pause / Cancel as options)
- target_cosfan_screen: Manage membership screen (single place to change plan, pause, or cancel)
- user_job: "Give me one clear place to adjust or stop my membership, and tell me exactly when the change takes effect."
- first_visible_promise: Header "Manage your membership" with an honesty line right under it: "Whatever you choose, it'll take effect on 3 March 2026. You'll still be able to watch until then." — the effective date and retained-access window are stated before any option is chosen.
- primary_cta: No single hard CTA — three equal, calmly-styled option cards (Change plan / Pause for a month / Cancel), each with a "+" expander and a one-line consequence label.
- secondary_cta: "Return to watching" / "What to watch before you leave" — a soft re-engagement prompt placed BELOW the options, not blocking them.
- section_order: (1) "Manage your membership" header → (2) effective-date + retained-access reassurance sentence → (3) "Member since" badge → (4) three option cards: Change plan ("Try a plan that's a better fit"), Pause for a month ("Keep your profiles and preferences"), Cancel ("Lose personalised recommendations") → (5) soft re-engagement strip.
- trust_elements: Up-front effective-date statement, "you'll still be able to watch until then" access reassurance, each option labeled with its honest consequence (including Cancel's downside stated plainly, not hidden), Pause offered as a middle path so users aren't forced into all-or-nothing.
- friction_removed: One hub instead of scattered settings; each option's consequence is pre-stated so users don't fear the unknown; Pause gives an alternative to cancelling (reduces churn honestly, not by obstruction); effective date removes "did I lose access immediately?" anxiety.
- empty_loading_error_handling: Change-plan sub-screen ("Change Plan") lists plans with effective-date note ("Your new plan will apply starting Jun 8, 2021") and a "KEEP MY 12 MONTH PLAN" no-op escape so a user who opened it by mistake can back out cleanly.
- monetization_exposure: Low — retention is attempted via a genuine Pause option and honest consequence labels, NOT via guilt loops, hidden cancel buttons, or multi-step mazes. This is the ethical edge of the dark-pattern line.
- onboarding_step: Post-purchase management; not onboarding.
- mobile_pattern: Option-hub with equal-weight expandable cards; effective-date reassurance header; Pause-as-alternative; soft re-engagement below (never blocking) the cancel path.
- why_relevant_to_cosfan: Directly models CosFan's "manage membership" + honest cancellation mandate. The screen "says" the effective date and access window first, frames Cancel as a legitimate equal option, and offers Pause as a humane middle path. Persuasion to stay is present but ethical (a real Pause + honest trade-off copy), which is exactly how CosFan should retain fans without dark patterns. The effective-date-first pattern and consequence-labeled options are must-adopt.
- what_to_learn: Lead with "your change takes effect on <date>, you keep access until then"; present Change/Pause/Cancel as equal calm options; label each option's real consequence; offer Pause as an honest churn alternative; keep any "stay" nudge soft and below the options.
- what_not_to_copy: Netflix brand chrome; "Lose personalised recommendations" is fine framing but CosFan must phrase consequences in its own honest words (e.g. "You'll lose access to <creator>'s member feed and your member-only channel"). Don't add more than a couple of retention steps.
- risk_notes: The single biggest dark-pattern risk lives here. CosFan must keep Cancel reachable in one tap, never add a forced phone-call/email-support step, never pre-tick a retained-charge, and ensure Pause genuinely pauses billing. If store-billing governs cancel, hand off honestly to the store sheet.
- MVP_action: Build manage-membership hub: header + "Changes take effect <date>; you keep access until then." → three pastel option rows (Change plan / Pause 1 month / Cancel membership), each with a plain consequence subtitle → optional soft "Not now, keep exploring <creator>" link below. Cancel leads to a single confirm (see Duolingo cancel ref), not a maze. Pause optional for MVP; Cancel + Change required.
