# Reference: Netflix - Active-plan + billing state (Account: Membership & Billing)
- source_app: Netflix
- mobbin_category: Subscription / Billing — account / billing status
- screen_type: Account screen with "Membership & Billing" + "Billing Details" sub-screen
- target_cosfan_screen: Active-plan + billing state screen (what plan am I on, when am I charged, how, and how do I stop)
- user_job: "Confirm what I'm paying, when the next charge hits, which card it uses, and give me a clear way to cancel."
- first_visible_promise: "Member since March 2023" up top (relationship framing), then a "MEMBERSHIP & BILLING" card that immediately answers the three core questions: payment method (masked card), the literal sentence "Your next billing date is 16 April 2023.", and a "Billing details >" drill-in.
- primary_cta: "Cancel Membership" — presented plainly as a normal full-width neutral button inside the billing card, not hidden, not styled as scary-red, not buried in a web redirect.
- secondary_cta: "Billing details >" row (drills into plan price, next billing date, and dated charge history) and "Manage account" for plan/payment changes.
- section_order: (1) "Member since" badge → (2) MEMBERSHIP & BILLING card: account email, masked payment method, plain-language next-billing-date sentence, "Billing details >", "Cancel Membership" → (3) PLAN DETAILS card (plan name e.g. "Standard with ads"). Billing Details sub-screen: Plan + "USD 6.99/month (pre-tax)" + "Next billing date September 6, 2024" + note that fees post a few days later + sales-tax caveat + dated line-item history.
- trust_elements: Exact next-billing-date as a sentence (not buried in fine print), masked card with brand (Visa/Mastercard •••• 0687), pre-tax price labeled "(pre-tax)" with an explicit sales-tax note, dated billing history (one year), "Member since" tenure.
- friction_removed: All three "what/when/how" billing facts are answered without tapping; cancellation is one tap from the billing card; after cancelling, the screen flips to a clear blue banner "Your membership will be cancelled at the end of your current billing period" and the button becomes "Restart Membership" — state is always legible.
- empty_loading_error_handling: Cancelled state = persistent info banner + changed end-date sentence ("Your membership will end on 15 April 2023") + "Restart Membership" affordance, so a cancelled user is never confused about access window or how to come back.
- monetization_exposure: Low/neutral — this is a management surface, not a sell. Honesty is the product: price, tax, date, card all exposed; re-subscribe offered without dark patterns.
- onboarding_step: Post-purchase account management; not onboarding.
- mobile_pattern: Card-based account layout; plain-language billing-date sentence; masked-card row; drill-in to dated billing history; state-swap of primary button (Cancel ↔ Restart) with status banner.
- why_relevant_to_cosfan: This is the gold standard for honest active-plan + billing state and exactly CosFan's "transparent recurring membership" mandate. The screen "says" the membership facts in human sentences and keeps Cancel one tap away — reducing the fear of "am I being silently charged / can I get out?" that kills B2C trust. The cancelled-state banner + Restart pattern is directly reusable. Persuasion is replaced by clarity, which is the right move for a fan-facing app avoiding sleazy connotations.
- what_to_learn: State the next charge date as a full sentence; show masked card + brand; label price pre-tax and note tax; keep Cancel visible (not hidden); after cancel, show a status banner + access-end date + an easy Restart; provide dated billing history.
- what_not_to_copy: Netflix's black/red brand chrome, the "visit netflix.com to change your plan" web-redirect dodge (CosFan should let users manage in-app per platform billing rules), and the dense all-caps section labels. Keep CosFan white/pastel and in-app.
- risk_notes: If CosFan uses App Store / Play billing, cancellation must route to the correct store sheet honestly (don't fake an in-app cancel that doesn't actually cancel store billing) — mirror Duolingo's "cancel in the App Store" clarity. Never obscure the renewal date.
- MVP_action: Build active-plan screen as two pastel cards: (1) Membership card — creator + tier name, "Renews on <date>", masked card row, "Manage / Cancel"; (2) Billing card — price "$X/mo (before tax)", next charge date, "View billing history". On cancel, swap to mint/lilac info banner "Your membership ends <date> — you keep access until then" + "Resume membership" button. Keep all copy in plain sentences.
