# Reference: Duolingo - Plan selection (annual vs monthly, Change Plan)
- source_app: Duolingo
- mobbin_category: Subscription / Billing — plan selection
- screen_type: Plan picker sheet (full-screen "Choose a plan" / "Change Plan")
- target_cosfan_screen: Plan selection screen (choose membership term before subscribing, and change-plan from active membership)
- user_job: "Show me the price options side by side so I can pick the term that fits my budget without feeling tricked."
- first_visible_promise: A clear "Choose a plan" header; on trial variants, an explicit free-trial duration banner ("7 DAY FREE TRIAL" / "Cancel anytime in the App Store") sits directly above the cards so the commitment is legible before any tap.
- primary_cta: Single bottom button that restates the exact commitment — "START MY FREE WEEK" (trial) or "KEEP MY 12 MONTH PLAN" (change-plan). One primary action, full-width, high-contrast.
- secondary_cta: Low-emphasis text link — "GO BACK" / "VIEW ALL PLANS" / "CANCEL SUBSCRIPTION" (on the change-plan variant). Never a competing colored button.
- section_order: (1) Back/close + optional plan badge → (2) Header "Choose a plan" → (3) Trial-duration label → (4) Stacked plan cards: Family / Individual / Monthly, each card = term name + per-month price on the right + total + savings strike-through → (5) "MOST POPULAR" badge on the recommended card + checkmark to show selection → (6) "Cancel anytime in the App Store" reassurance line → (7) Primary CTA → (8) Fine-print auto-renew terms at the very bottom.
- trust_elements: Per-month price shown even for annual ("$6.99 / MO" with "$83.99" total), strike-through original price ("$155.88 $83.99") to make the discount honest and verifiable, explicit "Cancel anytime in the App Store" line, full auto-renew disclosure in fine print ("automatically renews... cancel at least 24 hours prior").
- friction_removed: Comparing terms requires no math — every card normalizes to a per-month figure; the recommended choice is pre-selected with a checkmark; commitment length and trial length are stated before purchase, not after.
- empty_loading_error_handling: Post-purchase success uses a simple native-style alert ("You're all set. Your purchase was successful." → OK) rather than a celebratory hard-sell interstitial; selection state is a clear checkmark + border highlight.
- monetization_exposure: High — this is the conversion moment. But exposure is honest: prices, totals, discounts and renewal terms are all on one screen; no hidden "then $X" surprises.
- onboarding_step: Can appear post-onboarding (first paywall) or later from Manage Subscription. Not gated as step 1.
- mobile_pattern: Vertically stacked selectable price cards with a single sticky bottom CTA; "MOST POPULAR" badge + checkmark selection; per-unit price normalization; trial-duration banner.
- why_relevant_to_cosfan: This is the cleanest template for CosFan's plan selection. Each card "says" its own term + true monthly cost + total, so a fan choosing a creator membership can compare honestly. The recommended tier is nudged via a soft badge (persuasion by social proof, not pressure). It is placed before checkout so the fan commits with full knowledge — the exact "transparent recurring membership" posture CosFan wants, and the opposite of per-item PPV unlock. Highly reusable as structure.
- what_to_learn: Normalize every plan to a per-month number; always show the total too; use strike-through only when the discount is real; restate the precise commitment in the CTA label; keep "cancel anytime" visible at the decision point; one primary action only.
- what_not_to_copy: Duolingo's saturated gradient backgrounds, gem/mascot gamification, and the slightly pushy "JUST A BIT LONGER..." retention micro-copy. CosFan is white-first pastel, calm, collectible — not gamified.
- risk_notes: Avoid stacking too many tiers (Family/Individual/Monthly) if CosFan creators only offer 1-2 tiers — empty visual padding looks unfinished. Keep "MOST POPULAR" honest (don't badge the most expensive option by default). Ensure trial terms match App Store reality to avoid review rejection.
- MVP_action: Build plan selection as 1-3 stacked pastel cards (white card, lilac selected border, mint "Popular" badge). Each card: term name, large per-month price, smaller total + "billed monthly/yearly", optional honest savings note. Sticky bottom CTA restates commitment ("Start membership — $X/mo"). Persistent "Cancel anytime in Settings" line above CTA. Defer Family/group plans to post-MVP.
