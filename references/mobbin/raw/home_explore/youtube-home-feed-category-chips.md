# Reference: YouTube - Home feed with category filter chips

- source_app: YouTube (iOS)
- mobbin_category: Media / Video — home feed
- screen_type: Recommended feed of large thumbnail cards with a sticky category-chip rail
- target_cosfan_screen: Home feed (content from creators you follow + recommended), with category chips to refine
- user_job: "Give me a vertical stream of recommended content I can filter to a topic in one tap, each item clearly tied to the creator who made it."
- first_visible_promise: A horizontally scrolling chip rail (`All / iPhones / Security hackers / ...` — first chip preselected) sits pinned under the top bar; below it, large landscape thumbnail cards stack vertically, each with creator avatar + title + creator name + meta (views · age). Promise: "a personalized stream you can steer by topic, and you always know who made each piece."
- primary_cta: Tap a thumbnail card to open the content. Chips refine the whole feed in one tap.
- secondary_cta: Category chips (one-tap topic filter); per-card "..." overflow (save/not-interested); creator avatar tap → channel; bottom tab nav (Home / Shorts / Subscriptions / You).
- section_order: 1) Top bar (logo + cast/search) → 2) sticky horizontal category-chip rail → 3) vertical stack of large thumbnail cards, each = thumbnail + duration badge + avatar + title + creator + meta → 4) bottom tab nav.
- trust_elements: Every card binds content to a named creator (avatar + channel name) — strong authorship signal; view counts + upload age act as social proof and freshness; a small inline "is this a good suggestion?" feedback prompt (sibling screens) shows the feed listens.
- friction_removed: Category chips remove the need to navigate away or search to change topic — the feed reshapes in place. Large single-column thumbnails remove the squint of a dense grid. Consistent metadata layout makes every card scannable in the same glance.
- empty_loading_error_handling: Cards load with thumbnail placeholders; inline non-blocking banners (e.g. "Thanks for reporting", "Got it! Thanks for your feedback") appear within the feed rather than as interrupting modals — graceful inline state handling.
- monetization_exposure: Premium upsell appears in the top bar branding and via ads in-feed elsewhere; the home feed structure itself is not a paywall. Low-to-moderate, and avoidable in the pattern.
- onboarding_step: N/A steady-state.
- mobile_pattern: Sticky horizontal category-chip rail; single-column large landscape thumbnail cards; avatar + title + creator + meta block; duration/badge overlay; inline feedback banners; bottom tab nav.
- why_relevant_to_cosfan: Best reference for the "followed-creators + recommended" home feed where authorship matters. The chip rail says "steer this to your interest instantly"; each card's avatar+name says "this is a specific creator you can follow/subscribe to"; meta says "fresh and liked by others." Friction reduced: topic-switching is one tap and in place; every card is consistently scannable. It drives "open content" and, via the always-present avatar, "go to creator / follow." For CosFan's idol/cosplay creators, the single-column large-thumbnail card is a strong alternative to a grid when a post deserves a hero image (e.g. a new photoset or photocard reveal). Reusable as the chip-rail + authored-card pattern; must-modify away from video (no durations/play-centric framing unless CosFan has video) and into the pastel collectible aesthetic.
- what_to_learn: (1) A sticky category-chip rail is the lowest-friction way to let a single feed serve many tastes — adopt for CosFan genres/characters. (2) Bind every feed item to a creator (avatar + name) so follow/subscribe is always one tap away. (3) Show light freshness/social-proof meta to build trust without clutter. (4) Handle feedback/report states as inline non-blocking banners, not modals.
- what_not_to_copy: Video-specific chrome (duration badges, play overlays, Shorts tab) unless CosFan ships video; YouTube's dark, dense, ad-adjacent feel; the aggressive recommendation density. CosFan stays lighter and calmer.
- risk_notes: A pure algorithmic recommendation feed can drift toward attention-maximizing / suggestive content — CosFan must keep the followed-creators emphasis primary and apply safety curation to any "recommended" injection, so home reads as "my fandoms" not "an endless content casino." Avoid anything resembling full-screen vertical autoplay (Shorts) which collides with CosFan's anti-livestream/anti-TikTok stance.
- MVP_action: Build CosFan home as a sticky pastel category-chip rail over a feed that leads with content from creators you follow (recommended clearly secondary + safety-curated). Use large single-column hero cards for marquee posts (photoset/photocard drops) and reserve the 2-col masonry for explore. Every card carries creator avatar + name + light freshness meta; tap avatar → creator+follow. Handle report/hide inline, not via modal. No video/play chrome unless needed. Reuse chip rail + authored cards + inline states, restyle to pastel, keep followed-first and curated.
